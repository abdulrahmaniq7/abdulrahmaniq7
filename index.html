Questions / Before we build

Software services.
A clear starting point.

What software development services does Abdul offer?

Scope Laravel and PHP backend development, React and Next.js web applications, Flutter mobile applications, REST API integrations, custom ERP and CRM architecture, legacy modernization, and AI-agent pilot engagements.

Can we discuss a gated community management platform?

Yes. The proposed modular platform explores visitor management, security workspaces, smart access, facility booking, complaints, payments, notifications and AI-assisted operations. It is a product concept open to pilot discussions. Features and integrations are agreed after requirements analysis.

Does the community platform support residential and commercial properties?

The concept includes residential communities, commercial spaces and multiple properties with different roles and responsibilities. Regional branding, multilingual experiences and configurable workflows are planned for Asia, Europe and the Middle East. These are proposed capabilities, not a claim of current availability.

Can we work remotely with Abdul?

Abdul is based in Bengaluru, India and is open to remote collaboration. Share your project requirements, existing systems and preferred meeting time to discuss a suitable engagement.
